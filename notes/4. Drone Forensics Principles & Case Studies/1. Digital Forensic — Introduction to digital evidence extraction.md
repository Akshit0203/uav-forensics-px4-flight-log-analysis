# Digital Forensics — Introduction to Digital Evidence Extraction

> **Session Outcome:** Understand preservation, documentation, chain of custody, and how to handle drone-related evidence responsibly — including identifying crucial data/logs for flight data recording and retrieval.

---

# What is Digital Forensics?

Digital forensics is a discipline of forensic science that employs **scientific methods** for the **extraction and reconstruction** of a sequence of events related to a digital incident from one or more digital devices, in order to **prove or refute a case in a court of law**.

It includes: **Identification → Recovery → Investigation → Validation → Presentation** of facts regarding digital evidence found on computers or similar digital storage media devices.

---

## Criminal vs Civil Investigations

| Type | Description | Example (Drone Context) |
|---|---|---|
| **Criminal Cases** | Alleged violation of laws; handled by law enforcement and digital forensic examiners | A drone intentionally hits a person → FIR filed → prove the operator's identity and intent |
| **Civil Cases** | Protection of rights and property, or contractual disputes; may involve **electronic discovery (eDiscovery)** | A drone takes photos of someone on their private property → privacy violation claim |

### Real-World Context

- **Russia vs Lithuania:** Russia intentionally flies low-cost hobby drones near neighboring countries to observe response capabilities. Some have flown directly in front of commercial aircraft.
- **Defense espionage:** Suspects have flown drones to spy on military installations. Even after deleting video from the SD card, forensic recovery proved the surveillance activity.
- **DJI serial number tracing:** In Western countries, investigators traced a DJI drone's serial number → telemetry logs from the recovered transmitter → eBay purchase records → confirmed the buyer/operator.

# Digital Evidence

> **"Any data that is recorded or preserved on any medium in or by a computer system or other similar device."**
> Digital evidence is now used to prosecute **all types of crimes**, not just e-crime.

### Drone as a Cyber-Physical System

A drone is not just a cyber device — it is a **cyber-physical system** that physically flies, carries payloads, and can cause physical harm. Digital evidence from drones can prosecute physical crimes (assault, espionage, smuggling), not just cyber fraud.

## Types of Digital Data

| Type | Definition | Drone Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| **Persistent Data** | Remains intact when the device is powered off | Flight logs on **SD card**, onboard flash memory (historically 256 KB, now up to 2 GB) — parallel to a hard disk |
| **Volatile Data** | Lost when the device is powered off | Working **RAM** on the microcontroller — stores real-time computations (waypoints, mathematical equations during flight) |

# 5 Rules of Evidence

These apply to **all evidence** in law (not just digital), and are a core part of cyber law.

| # | Rule | Meaning | Drone Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Admissible** | Must be legally fit to be used in court | Evidence must be collected following proper procedures |
| 2 | **Authentic** | Must relate directly to the incident in a relevant way | Flight logs must be from the specific drone in question |
| 3 | **Complete** | Must include exculpatory evidence for alternative suspects (freed from blame) | Consider all possible operators, not just the suspect |
| 4 | **Reliable** | No question about authenticity and veracity | **Chain of custody** must be maintained — photo/video the handover process |
| 5 | **Believable** | Clear, easy to understand, and convincing to a jury | Reports must be well-structured and non-technical for judges/jury |

> **Chain of Custody:** In the forensic lab, whenever an artifact is received, take **photos and video recordings** of the person handing it over to ensure complete reliability.

# Why Digital Forensics Works — Data is Very Hard to Kill

| Principle | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Deleted files aren't securely deleted** | Recovery is possible + you can determine **when** a file was deleted |
| **Renaming files is pointless** | File metadata and content remain unchanged on SD cards/memory chips |
| **Formatting doesn't delete data** | Most underlying data survives a format operation |
| **Web-based email recovery** | Can be partially recovered directly from a computer |
| **Network file reassembly** | Files transferred over a network can be reassembled as evidence |
| **Uninstalled app analysis** | More complex than expected, but traces remain |
| **Volatile data persists** | Hangs around for some time, even across reboots (specialized capture tools exist, though expensive) |
| **Application remnants** | Previously executed applications leave recoverable traces |
| **Encrypted evidence** | Difficult but possible — requires paid tools for decryption. Token harvesting, directory discovery, and key extraction are used for cases involving dark-web encryption. |
| **Anti-forensics software** | Mostly broken now — it's a cat-and-mouse game similar to electronic warfare |

> **Basic enabler: Data is very hard to permanently destroy.** At a certain level, and for a certain price, everything is recoverable.

### Real-World Example

A suspect flew a drone to spy on a military installation. They deleted the videos from the SD card. Forensic recovery retrieved the deleted videos, proving the drone was used for defense surveillance → suspect apprehended.

# Exam / Interview Points

### Digital Forensics Definition
- Scientific extraction & reconstruction of events from digital devices → prove/refute a case in court
- 5 phases: **Identify → Recover → Investigate → Validate → Present**

### Evidence Types
- **Persistent** = survives power-off (SD card, flash memory) — like a hard disk
- **Volatile** = lost on power-off (RAM) — real-time computations
- A drone is a **cyber-physical system** — digital evidence can prosecute physical crimes

### 5 Rules of Evidence
- **Admissible, Authentic, Complete, Reliable, Believable**
- **Chain of custody** = documented, unbroken evidence trail (photo/video handover)

### Why Forensics Works
- Deleted ≠ destroyed (files recoverable + deletion timestamp)
- Formatting ≠ deletion (most data survives)
- Renaming ≠ hiding (metadata unchanged)
- Anti-forensics software = mostly broken
- **Data is very hard to kill**

### Key Cases
- DJI serial number → transmitter telemetry → eBay records → suspect identified
- Deleted spy videos → forensic recovery from SD card → military espionage proven
- Russia flies hobby drones near Lithuanian/NATO airspace to test response
