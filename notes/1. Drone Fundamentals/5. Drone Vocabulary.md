# Drone Vocabulary

## Alt Hold (Altitude Hold)

In **altitude hold mode**, the copter maintains a **consistent altitude** while allowing roll, pitch, and yaw to be controlled normally.

- The **throttle is automatically controlled** to maintain the current altitude.
- The pilot retains manual control over roll, pitch, and yaw.

## RTH (Return to Home)

A feature that allows the quadcopter to **automatically return to a predefined home point** from any position.

- **GPS is mandatory** for RTH to function.
- RTH is also triggered automatically in case of:
  - **Signal disconnection** (failsafe)
  - **Battery drain** (low battery failsafe)

> **Forensic Relevance:** RTH logs reveal the operator's launch point, which is critical evidence for identifying the pilot's location.

---

## Hovering

Maintaining a **fixed position in the air** (constant altitude and GPS coordinates) without any lateral movement.

---

## Loitering

Flying in a **repeated pattern or circling around a point of interest**. Often associated with surveillance or reconnaissance activity.

> **Forensic Relevance:** Loitering patterns in flight logs can indicate surveillance intent or target observation.

---

## Stable Mode

A flight mode where the drone **auto-levels itself** using gyroscope and accelerometer data, making it easier to fly for beginners. The drone returns to a level hover when control sticks are released.

---

## Geofencing

A **software-enforced virtual boundary** that prevents the drone from flying into restricted areas (e.g., airports, military zones, no-fly zones).

- Uses **GPS coordinates** to define boundaries.
- The drone will **refuse to enter or take off** within a geofenced area.
- Manufacturers like DJI enforce geofencing via firmware.

> **Forensic Relevance:** Geofencing bypass or modification in firmware is evidence of intentional rule violation.

---

## Waypoints

**Pre-programmed GPS coordinates** that define an autonomous flight path. The drone flies from waypoint to waypoint without manual pilot input.

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704214826.png)

> **Forensic Relevance:** Waypoint data extracted from the flight controller reveals the **planned route**, which may differ from the actual flight path — both are critical evidence.

# Forensics Context

## What is Forensic Science?

Forensic science is the application of **scientific methods and principles** to resolve civil disputes, enforce criminal laws, and ensure compliance with government regulations. It bridges the gap between science and the legal system, ensuring that evidence is **collected, preserved, and analyzed** to find the truth in a court of law.

## Digital / Cyber Forensics

**Drone Forensics** is a **sub-branch of Digital Forensics**.

Key principles:
1. **Chain of Custody** — Every piece of evidence must have a documented, unbroken chain of custody from seizure to courtroom.
2. **Device Return Obligation** — Unlike other forensic artifacts, digital devices must be **handed back to the issuing agency in the same state** (no alteration or damage).
