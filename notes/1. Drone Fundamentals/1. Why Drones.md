# Why Drones?

## Definition

A **drone**, also known as an **Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV)** or **Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS)**, is an aircraft that operates **without an onboard human pilot**. It can be remotely controlled by an operator or fly autonomously using onboard software, GPS, sensors, and a flight controller.

Drones have transformed multiple industries by providing a **cost-effective, efficient, safe, and flexible** alternative to traditional methods of aerial operations.

> **Exam Point:** UAV refers only to the aircraft, whereas **UAS** includes the UAV, ground control station, communication link, and payload.

---

# Why are Drones Becoming Important?

Drones are increasingly used because they can:

- Access difficult or dangerous locations
- Reduce operational costs
- Perform repetitive tasks autonomously
- Collect real-time aerial data
- Increase safety by reducing human exposure to hazards
- Improve speed and operational efficiency

## 2. Iran Conflict – Drones Take Center Stage

Recent conflicts involving Iran have highlighted the extensive use of drones for long-range operations.

### Applications

- Long-range surveillance
- One-way attack drones
- Precision targeting
- Border monitoring
- Strategic reconnaissance

### Importance

- Lower operational cost
- Long endurance

Recovered drones help investigators determine:

- Flight route
- Launch location
- Payload type

## 3. Agriculture (Agro Drones / Drone Didi)

Agricultural drones improve farming efficiency through precision agriculture.

### Applications

- Crop monitoring
- Fertilizer spraying
- Pesticide spraying
- Seed distribution
- Irrigation assessment
- Crop health analysis using multispectral cameras

### Benefits

- Saves time
- Reduces chemical wastage
- Improves crop yield
- Covers large fields quickly
- Reduces manual labor

### Drone Didi Initiative

**Drone Didi** is an Indian government initiative that trains women from Self-Help Groups (SHGs) to operate agricultural drones for fertilizer and pesticide spraying, promoting precision farming and rural employment.

## 4. Flying Taxis (eVTOL)

Flying taxis use **Electric Vertical Take-Off and Landing (eVTOL)** technology to transport passengers within urban environments.

### Features

- Vertical takeoff and landing
- Electric propulsion
- Autonomous or semi-autonomous operation
- Reduced traffic congestion
- Environmentally friendly

### Applications

- Urban transportation

- Air taxi services

# Why This Matters in Drone Forensics ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Understanding drone applications helps investigators identify:

- **Purpose of the flight** (e.g., agriculture, surveying, photography, logistics, or surveillance).
- **Expected hardware** (camera, LiDAR, sprayer, delivery mechanism, thermal sensor).
- **Expected flight patterns** (grid patterns for surveying, repeated routes for deliveries, loitering for surveillance).
- **Potential evidence sources** such as flight logs, GPS tracks, telemetry data, onboard storage, and payload components.

Knowing the intended application makes it easier to determine whether a recovered drone was being used **legitimately or for unlawful activities**.

## Key Takeaways

- Drones are versatile platforms used across civilian, commercial, and military sectors.
- Their widespread use is driven by efficiency, safety, automation, and cost savings.
- Understanding why drones are used helps forensic investigators reconstruct flight purpose, identify evidence, and determine whether a drone was used lawfully or maliciously.
