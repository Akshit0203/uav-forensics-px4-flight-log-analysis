# 1.1 UAV vs Drone vs Helicopter vs Quadcopter

| Term | Full Form | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **UAV** | Unmanned Aerial Vehicle | An aircraft with no human pilot, crew, or passengers on board. Controlled remotely or autonomously using onboard systems and pre-programmed flight paths. |
| **Drone** | Unmanned / Remotely Operated Vehicle | A broader term covering any unmanned, remotely operated or autonomous vehicle — including land rovers (UGV), aquatic drones, and UAVs. |
| **Helicopter** | — | A type of rotorcraft in which lift and thrust are supplied by horizontally spinning rotors. |
| **Quadcopter** | Quad = 4 Rotors | An aerial vehicle with 4 rotors arranged in a horizontal plane. |

> **Key Distinction:** UAV specifically refers to aerial unmanned vehicles. Drone is a broader umbrella term that also includes ground (UGV) and aquatic unmanned vehicles.

# 1.2 Classification

## By Weight (AUW – All Up Weight)

| Category | Weight Range |
|---|---|
| **Nano** | Under 250g |
| **Micro** | 250g – 2kg |
| **Small** | 2kg – 25kg |
| **Medium** | 25kg – 150kg |
| **Large** | Over 150kg |

## By Application

- **UCAV** – Unmanned Combat Aerial Vehicle (military / armed drones)
- **Drone** – General-purpose unmanned vehicle (civilian, commercial, etc.)

# 1.3 Regulatory Framework (India – DGCA)

Drone rules in India are regulated by the **Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA)**. Registration is mandatory for almost all drones on the **DigitalSky Platform**, and operators must obtain a **Remote Pilot Certificate**.

### Registration

- All drones weighing **above 250g** require a **Unique Identification Number (UIN)** issued via the DigitalSky platform.

### Pilot License

- Except for **Nano drones** used for **non-commercial purposes**, operators must obtain a **Remote Pilot Certificate/License** after completing training at a **DGCA-approved Remote Pilot Training Organization (RPTO)**.

### Airspace Zoning (DigitalSky Interactive Map)

| Zone | Rules |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Green Zone** | Free to fly up to **400 feet** without prior permission. |
| 🟡 **Yellow Zone** | Requires **prior permission / Air Defense Clearance**. |
| 🔴 **Red Zone** | Strictly **"No-Fly Zone"** (near airports, military installations, international borders). |

### Operational Limits

- Drones must always be flown within **Visual Line of Sight (VLOS)**.
- Maximum flying altitude: **400 feet (120 meters)** above ground level.

### NPNT Rule

> **No Permission, No Takeoff (NPNT):** India enforces this protocol — the drone will **physically not take off** unless authorized via the DigitalSky digital platform.

## NPNT (No Permission, No Takeoff)

**Definition:**  
NPNT (No Permission, No Takeoff) is a safety mechanism introduced by the DGCA under India's Drone Rules. It is designed so that **applicable drones** cannot take off unless they receive digital flight authorization through the DigitalSky platform.

### Does every drone require NPNT?

**No.**

The rules depend on the drone category.

|Drone Category|Registration Required|NPNT Applies?|
|---|---|---|
|**Nano (≤ 250 g)**|**No** (for typical non-commercial use)|**Generally No**|
|**Micro (>250 g – 2 kg)**|Yes|Yes|
|**Small, Medium, Large**|Yes|Yes|

### Example

✅ **DJI Neo (≈135 g)** or another nano hobby drone:

- No registration required for normal recreational use.
- NPNT authorization is generally **not required**.
- You **must still follow** airspace restrictions, altitude limits, and avoid prohibited areas.

❌ **DJI Mini 4 Pro (~249 g)** (or any drone that falls outside the exempt category based on applicable certification/rules):

- Registration and regulatory requirements may apply depending on its classification and use.
- It must comply with the applicable DGCA requirements before flying.

---

### Important Note ⭐

Even if your drone is exempt from registration or NPNT, **that does not mean you can fly anywhere**.

You still have to comply with:

- Airspace restrictions (Green, Yellow, Red Zones)
- Maximum permitted altitude
- No-fly zones (airports, military installations, etc.)
- Privacy and public safety rules
- Other applicable DGCA Drone Rules.

# Exam / Interview Points

- **UAV** = Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (aerial only)
- **Drone** = Broader term (aerial, ground, aquatic)
- **UCAV** = Unmanned Combat Aerial Vehicle
- **AUW** = All Up Weight (basis for drone classification)
- Weight categories: Nano → Micro → Small → Medium → Large
- **DGCA** regulates drones in India
- **DigitalSky Platform** = registration & airspace verification
- **UIN** = Unique Identification Number (required for drones > 250g)
- **RPTO** = Remote Pilot Training Organization
- **NPNT** = No Permission, No Takeoff
- **VLOS** = Visual Line of Sight (mandatory operational requirement)
- Three airspace zones: Green (free), Yellow (permission needed), Red (no-fly)
- Max altitude: **400 feet / 120 meters** AGL
