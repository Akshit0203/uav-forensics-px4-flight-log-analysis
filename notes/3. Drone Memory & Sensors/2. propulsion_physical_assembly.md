# Software Configuration, Calibration & Final Assembly

> A quadcopter is a dead machine until we program it. The flight controller must be configured and calibrated before the drone can fly.

---

## Two Types of Quadcopter Control

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Manual** | Pilot controls the drone in real-time via FPV camera or Visual Line of Sight (VLOS). |
| **Autonomous** | All flight commands are pre-programmed. On power-on, the drone flies to waypoints (lat/long), adjusts altitude, and returns — all automatically. |

---

# Step 1: Connect Flight Controller to Laptop

- Connect your flight controller to a laptop using a **micro-USB data cable**.
- Open configuration software: **Mission Planner** or **QGroundControl**.

> ⚠️ **Use a true data cable**, not a charging cable. Charging cables lack internal data lines and will not transmit signals to the FC.

---

# Step 2: Motor Direction Test

> **Propellers must NOT be attached during motor testing.** Propellers are always the **absolute last** thing installed.

### How to Test

1. Run the **motor test** via Mission Planner / QGroundControl software.
2. Gently touch the side of the spinning motor with a finger to feel rotation direction.
3. Match each motor against the layout diagram:

| Position | Motor # | Required Rotation |
|---|---|---|
| Front Right | 1 | CCW ↺ |
| Rear Left | 2 | CCW ↺ |
| Front Left | 3 | CW ↻ |
| Rear Right | 4 | CW ↻ |

### If a Motor Spins the Wrong Way

**Swap any two of the three motor wires** connected to the ESC (keep the middle wire in place). This reverses the spin direction.

### Safe Testing Environment

- Use a **test bench** that clamps the quadcopter at its base while allowing vertical travel (safe for indoor testing).
- Otherwise, perform first powered tests in a **wide-open outdoor space**.

---

# Step 3: Mandatory Hardware Calibrations

Run these three calibrations through Mission Planner / QGroundControl:

1. **Accelerometer Calibration** — levels the flight controller
2. **Compass Calibration** — calibrates the magnetometer for accurate heading
3. **Radio / Transmitter Stick Calibration** — maps transmitter stick endpoints to FC inputs

---

# Step 4: Final Hardware Integration

## Strap the Battery

- Place a **3S or 4S 2200mAh LiPo** battery **centrally** on the frame.
- Secure tightly with a **Velcro strap** near the center of gravity.
- Connect via the **XT60 connector** soldered to the PDB (completed in the first assembly step).

### Battery Mounting Position

| Landing Gear? | Mount Position | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Tall landing gear** | **Underneath** the center plate | Clearance available below |
| **No landing gear** | **On top** of the top plate | If the drone flips and crashes, the motor/propeller height shields the battery from direct ground impact |

> ⚠️ **LiPo batteries are chemically sensitive and fragile.** A direct impact can cause rupture and **immediate fire**. Always protect the battery from crash impacts.

### Power-On Sequence

When the battery is connected and powered on, the ESCs will emit a **series of musical beeping sounds** during initialization — this is normal.

## Attach Propellers (LAST Step)

- Install **2 CW propellers** on motors 3 & 4 (front-left and rear-right).
- Install **2 CCW propellers** on motors 1 & 2 (front-right and rear-left).
- Secure using **prop adapters** or **self-locking nuts**.

> Propellers are mounted **only after** all motor tests, calibrations, and software setup are confirmed.

---

# Forensic Relevance

> Programming the flight controller through Mission Planner is crucial from a forensic perspective. The main goal is to understand **onboard data storage** — where flight data is recorded so we can extract it later and reconstruct the drone's takeoff point, flight path, and other details. **Flight logs are always recorded and available for post-flight forensic analysis.**

# Exam / Interview Points

- Two control types: **Manual** (FPV / VLOS) and **Autonomous** (pre-programmed waypoints)
- Always use a **data cable**, not a charging cable, to connect FC to laptop
- **Mission Planner** and **QGroundControl** = FC configuration software
- Motor test: **never with propellers attached**
- Motors 1 & 2 (FR + RL) = **CCW** | Motors 3 & 4 (FL + RR) = **CW**
- Wrong direction fix: **swap any 2 of 3 motor wires**
- Three mandatory calibrations: **Accelerometer, Compass, Radio sticks**
- Battery mounted **centrally** near CG, secured with Velcro strap
- No landing gear → mount battery **on top** (crash protection)
- LiPo rupture on impact → **fire risk**
- ESCs beep musical tones on power-on (normal initialization)
- Propellers = **absolute last step** after all calibrations
- Flight logs are always recorded → key forensic evidence source
