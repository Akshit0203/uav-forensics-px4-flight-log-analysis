# Drone Sensors & Stability Hardware

> Drone sensors are devices that measure **motion, orientation, altitude, and environmental data**. They provide the vital telemetry required by flight controllers to stabilize the aircraft, avoid obstacles, and navigate autonomously.

---

## Sensor Categories

| Category | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| **Motion** | How fast the drone is traveling (speed, acceleration) |
| **Orientation** | Compass direction (N, S, E, W) and heading |
| **Altitude** | Height via barometric pressure |
| **Environmental** | Obstacle detection, wind, temperature |

---

# Flight Stability & Navigation Sensors

> These are **compulsory** for any basic flight platform.

## 1. Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU)

The IMU is the most critical sensor — it tracks **tilt, rotation, and linear acceleration**, allowing the drone to stabilize even in windy conditions.

| Sub-Sensor | Axes | Measures | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gyroscope** | Triple-axis | Angular velocity (rotation rate) | Detects tilt and spin |
| **Accelerometer** | Triple-axis | Linear acceleration | Detects movement and orientation relative to gravity |
| **Magnetometer** | Triple-axis | Magnetic field | Compass heading (see below) |

> ⚠️ **Without an IMU, consumer drones would flip or drift instantly** — the IMU is what keeps the drone level and stable.

---

## 2. Magnetometer (Electronic Compass)

Acts as an **electronic compass**, allowing the drone to orient itself relative to the **Earth's magnetic field**.

- Provides accurate **heading data** (which direction the drone is facing).
- Without this sensor, navigation manoeuvres become highly imprecise and the drone **drifts off course**.

---

## 3. Barometer (Altitude Sensor)

Measures **atmospheric pressure** to estimate and maintain the drone's altitude.

- Works by measuring the ambient air pressure at the drone's current position.
- Enables stable altitude hold — critical for **aerial photography**.
- Altitude is inferred from pressure: higher altitude = lower air pressure.

---

# Exam / Interview Points

| Sensor | Measures | Without It |
|---|---|---|
| **IMU** | Speed, orientation, tilt (gyro + accelerometer + magnetometer) | Drone flips or drifts instantly |
| **Gyroscope** | Angular velocity (rotation) | Cannot detect tilt/spin |
| **Accelerometer** | Linear acceleration | Cannot detect movement direction |
| **Magnetometer** | Magnetic field (compass heading) | Imprecise navigation, drifts off course |
| **Barometer** | Atmospheric pressure → altitude | Cannot hold altitude |

- IMU = **Gyroscope + Accelerometer + Magnetometer** (all triple-axis)
- Barometer estimates altitude from **air pressure** (higher = lower pressure)
- All four sensors are **mandatory** for basic flight stability and navigation
