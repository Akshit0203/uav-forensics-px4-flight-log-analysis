# Quadcopter Main Body

The main body consists of three parts: **Frame**, **Power Distribution Board (PDB)**, and **Landing Gear**. In modern designs, the PDB is often integrated directly into the frame plates.

---

# 1. Power Distribution Board (PDB)

![232](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704233120.png)

The PDB has the same footprint as multirotor control boards and provides clean, tidy power distribution wiring.

- Uses **3.5mm male bullet connectors** (8 included) for ESC connections.
- The PCB is **gold-plated** for optimal efficiency.
- Features a **2-pin JST auxiliary power-out port** — perfect for powering LEDs (for night flying) or other accessories.

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704233244.png)

> The yellow port shown above is a **JST connector** — remember this term.

# 2. Quadcopter Frame

## F450 Frame

![245](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704233545.png)

- **"F450"** = diagonal motor-to-motor distance is **450 mm**.
- Cost: ~₹700.
- Lightweight, durable, easy to assemble.
- Compatible with various setups; ideal for aerial photography and beginner builds.
- Power distribution board is integrated into the central plate, with mounting holes for the arms.

## S500 Frame

![322](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704233602.png)

- Uses **carbon-fiber arms** and improved landing gear.
- Carbon-fiber legs are **flexible** — they splay outward up to 2 cm during a hard landing to absorb impact and cushion the electronics.
- Rigid, straight plastic legs (like on F450) often **snap on hard landings**.

## FPV Racing Frame

![269](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704234152.png)

- **FPV = First Person View** — the pilot flies by watching a real-time video feed from the drone's camera.
- FPV frames have a **dedicated camera mount in the nose**.
- Can be flown with or without landing gear (remove to save weight).
- Some frames include integrated **propeller guards**.

# 3. Electronic Speed Controller (ESC)

An ESC is an electronic circuit that varies a motor's speed by regulating the current delivered to it. Its throttle range can be configured to be compatible with all transmitters.

### ESC Connections

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704234410.png)

| Wire | Connects To |
|---|---|
| **Power leads** (+/-) | Power Distribution Board (battery side) |
| **3-wire signal cable** (thin) | Receiver or Flight Controller |
| **3 thick output wires** | BLDC Motor |

### Common ESC Models

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704234514.png)

![303](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704234603.png)

- **20A ESC** — Older models (e.g., Avionics), max continuous load 20A.
- **30A ESC** — Standard choice for quadcopters, ~₹400. Yellow body, bullet connectors.
- **SimonK ESC** — Named after the firmware designer. Highly reliable, smooth throttle transitions, enhanced stability.

### ESC Ratings & BEC vs UBEC

| Rating | Use Case |
|---|---|
| 20A, 30A, 40A | Standard quadcopters |
| 80A+ | Heavy-lift / high-power drones |

| Type | Full Form | Method | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BEC** | Battery Elimination Circuit | Linear regulator | Steps battery voltage down to 5V; dumps extra voltage as heat. **Avoid.** |
| **UBEC** | Universal BEC | Switching regulator | Handles high currents (>5A) and large voltage drops (35V → 5V). **Always prefer UBEC.** |

### ESC Throttle Calibration (Binding ESC to Tx)

Calibration ensures the transmitter's max and min throttle range is synchronized with the ESC/motor.

**Procedure:**

1. Turn on transmitter. Ensure throttle trims are at **neutral** and throttle endpoints (EPA) at **100%**.
2. Keep ESC powered **off**. Insert bind plug into receiver's BIND/BAT port. Power on ESC. Receiver LED flashes rapidly → turns solid = bind successful. Remove bind plug.
3. Turn on transmitter. Push throttle stick to **full throttle**.
4. Power on ESCs. Motors emit **3 short beeps** (beep, beep, beep).
5. Immediately pull throttle to **minimum**. Motor beeps once + continuous confirmation beep.
6. ESC has now memorized max/min throttle range. Turn off to save.

> This calibration can also be performed programmatically via **Mission Planner** software.

### ESC Checker / Tester

![261](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704235055.png)

A standalone **CCPM tester** (~₹1,000) that tests ESCs and servos without a transmitter or flight controller.

- Connects directly to the ESC's signal wire.
- Sends a PWM signal; you control motor speed by turning a knob.
- Verifies signal integrity, throttle response, and detects faulty hardware.

# 4. BLDC Motor (Brushless Direct Current)

![336](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704235243.png)

## Model Designation

Example: **A2212 / 1000KV**

- **22** = stator diameter (mm)
- **12** = stator height / number of magnet poles
- **1000KV** = KV rating

## KV Rating

The **KV rating** indicates the number of RPM per volt applied under **no-load conditions**.

| Motor | Battery | Voltage | No-Load RPM |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1000 KV | 3S LiPo | 11.1V | 11,100 RPM |
| 980 KV | 3S LiPo | 11.1V | 10,878 RPM |

## Low KV vs High KV

| | Low KV | High KV |
|---|---|---|
| **Winds** | More winds of thinner wire | Fewer winds of thicker wire |
| **Current** | Lower amps, higher volts | Higher amps, lower volts |
| **Torque** | High torque | Low torque |
| **Propeller** | Large prop, lower RPM | Small prop, high RPM |
| **Use Case** | Heavy-lift multirotors | FPV racing drones |

> **Important Rule:** If you build a quadcopter on 3S with 1045 propellers, and upgrade to a 4S battery, you **must downsize** the propellers (e.g., to 8045 or 8035) to avoid drawing too much current and burning out the motor.

# 5. Propeller

## Specification Format

Propellers are specified as **diameter × pitch** (both in inches).

| Spec | Diameter | Pitch | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1045** | 10 inches | 4.5 inches | Standard, commonly used |
| 9035 | 9 inches | 3.5 inches | Slower, more stable — ideal for beginners |
| 5x3 | 5 inches | 3 inches | Small FPV props |

- **Pitch** = the theoretical forward distance per revolution in a solid medium (e.g., 4.5-inch pitch = climbs 4.5 inches per revolution).

## CW vs CCW Propellers

![257](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704235835.png)

![275](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704235938.png)

- Propellers come in **CW (clockwise)** and **CCW (counter-clockwise)** pairs.
- Color-coded caps for easy identification:

| Cap Color | Rotation |
|---|---|
| **Black** | Clockwise (CW) |
| **Silver / Red / Crimson** | Counter-clockwise (CCW) |

> **Key Point:** Motors always spin in one direction — it is **only the propeller blade shape** that is CW or CCW. This is a simple mechanical keying system.

## Propeller Guards

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260705000035.png)

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260705000044.png)

Propeller guards are lightweight, flexible plastic rings that encircle spinning blades.

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Safety** | Physical barrier preventing blades from striking people, pets, or objects |
| **Damage Prevention** | Absorbs impact from walls, branches, furniture — keeps drone airborne |
| **Beginner Confidence** | Peace of mind for new pilots learning controls |

> **Tip:** Always keep spare propellers on hand. Crashes are common when starting out.

# 6. Flight Controller (FC) — The Brain

![490](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260705000310.png)

![397](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260705000342.png)

The flight controller stabilizes the aircraft, ensures precise manoeuvres, and serves as the hub for all peripherals (ESC, GPS, LED, servos, receiver, FPV camera, VTX).

## Firmware Stacks (Software)

| Stack | Origin | Strengths |
|---|---|---|
| **PX4** | Dronecode Foundation (open-source) | Precision, reliability, modularity, advanced autonomous capabilities |
| **ArduPilot** | Open-source community | Versatility, ease of use across various platforms (planes + multirotors) |

> ⚠️ **Don't confuse software and hardware:** PX4 and ArduPilot are **firmware stacks** (software). Pixhawk and APM are **physical flight controller boards** (hardware).

## Hardware Evolution

| Generation | Processor | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| APM | 8-bit | Legacy, unsupported for ~7 years |
| F4 | STM32 F4 | Older generation |
| F7 | STM32 F7 | Improved processing |
| **H7** | **STM32 H7 (32-bit)** | **Current best — superior processing, extremely precise control** |

## Built-in Sensors

| Sensor | Function |
|---|---|
| **Triple-axis Gyroscope** | Measures angular rotation |
| **Triple-axis Accelerometer** | Measures acceleration |
| **Barometer** | Determines altitude |
| **Magnetometer (Compass)** | Measures heading |

### Attitude Control

Gyros and accelerometers sense attitudinal changes and instruct ESCs to vary motor speed accordingly. All Roll, Pitch, and Yaw adjustments are governed by varying motor speed through ESCs.

## Choosing an Autopilot Board

| Factor | Details |
|---|---|
| **Sensor Redundancy** | Multiple IMUs on one board — if one fails, the other takes over |
| **Servo/Motor Outputs** | Number of motors/servos supported |
| **UARTs** | Ports for telemetry radios, GPS, companion computers |
| **External Buses** | I2C, CAN bus — for LiDAR, ultrasonic, rangefinders, airspeed sensors, LEDs |
| **Analog I/O** | For RSSI, battery voltage/current, other analog sensors |
| **Integrated OSD** | On-Screen Display overlays heading, altitude, battery, GPS on the video feed |
| **Vibration Isolation** | Internal dampening suspension for IMU — filters high-frequency motor vibrations (standard in DJI) |
| **IMU Heaters** | For extreme cold environments (Russia, Scandinavia) — on-board temperature control |

# 7. GPS Module

![613](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260705000820.png)

- GPS modules are mounted on a **tall mast** (~6 inches above the frame) to move the antenna away from electromagnetic noise from the receiver, ESCs, and battery.
- Newer modules (**M8N, M9N, 10N**) include an **integrated magnetometer (compass)**. Older 7-series models did not.
- The compass must be mounted **away from high-current wiring** to minimize electromagnetic interference.

# 8. Required Accessories

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260705000958.png)

| Accessory | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CG Balancer** | Balances the center of gravity | Battery (heaviest component) must be placed at the CG. Hold opposite arms and check for tilt in both directions. |
| **Servo** | Controls gimbal, payload drop | — |
| **Allen Key / Hex Key Set** | Mounting motors and frame parts | Essential toolkit |
| **LiPo Voltage Tester** | Checks individual cell voltages | ~₹400–500. Connects to balance leads. Recharge when 3S drops below 10V total. |
| **Battery Charger (IMAX B6)** | Safe LiPo charging | ~₹5,000–6,000 |

### Battery Charger — IMAX B6

![318](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260705001247.png)

- A **digital balance charger** supporting LiPo, Li-ion, LiFe, and NiMH batteries from **1S to 6S**.
- Features **cell-balancing** and **strict cut-off voltages**.
- Requires manual input of battery settings.
- **Industry standard** for RC enthusiasts.

> ⚠️ **LiPo batteries pose severe fire risks if overcharged or damaged.** Investing in a quality charger is critical.

# Exam / Interview Points

### Frame
- **F450** = 450mm diagonal, ~₹700, standard beginner frame
- **S500** = carbon-fiber arms/legs, flexible landing gear absorbs impact
- **FPV** = First Person View — pilot flies via real-time video feed

### ESC
- **SimonK** = popular firmware for ESCs
- **BEC** (linear) vs **UBEC** (switching) — always prefer UBEC
- ESC throttle calibration: Tx on → full throttle → power ESC → 3 beeps → pull to min → 1 beep = done
- **CCPM tester** = standalone ESC/servo checker

### Motor
- **BLDC** = Brushless DC Motor
- **KV** = RPM per volt (no-load)
- Low KV = high torque, big prop (heavy lift) | High KV = high RPM, small prop (racing)
- Model number: first 2 digits = stator diameter, last 2 = stator height

### Propeller
- Spec = **diameter × pitch** (inches)
- **1045** = 10-inch diameter, 4.5-inch pitch (standard)
- CW cap = black | CCW cap = silver/red
- Motors spin one direction — blade shape determines CW/CCW

### Flight Controller
- **PX4** (software) ≠ **Pixhawk** (hardware)
- **ArduPilot** (software) ≠ **APM** (hardware)
- Evolution: 8-bit APM → F4 → F7 → **H7** (current)
- Core sensors: gyroscope, accelerometer, barometer, magnetometer
- **OSD** = On-Screen Display (flight data overlaid on video)
- High-end FCs: vibration isolation, IMU heaters, sensor redundancy

### GPS
- Mounted on tall mast (~6 inches) to avoid EMI
- Modern modules (M8N, M9N, 10N) include integrated compass
- Compass must be away from high-current wires

### Accessories
- **CG Balancer** — battery must sit at center of gravity
- **IMAX B6** — industry standard charger, supports 1S–6S, multi-chemistry
- LiPo voltage tester: recharge 3S pack when total drops below **10V**
