# 1. Battery (LiPo – Lithium Polymer)

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704232235.png)

## Voltage & Cell Count ("S" Rating)

Each LiPo cell is rated at **3.7V**. The total voltage is determined by the number of cells connected in **series**, denoted by the **"S" count**.

| S Count | Cells | Voltage | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1S | 1 | 3.7V | Tiny/micro drones |
| 2S | 2 | 7.4V | Small drones |
| **3S** | **3** | **11.1V** | **Standard quadcopter (~30 min flight)** |
| 4S | 4 | 14.8V | Performance drones |
| 6S | 6 | 22.2V | Heavy agricultural drones (5–10L spray payload) |

> You can physically see the individual cells inside a battery pack — each cell is an independent unit wrapped in aluminum, encased in PVC, and bundled together with a main connector.

### Voltage–Motor KV Relationship

Battery voltage directly determines motor specifications:

- **Motor KV rating** = RPM per volt supplied.
- A **1,000 KV motor** on a 3S (11.1V) battery → spins at ~11,100 RPM.
- The same motor on a 6S (22.2V) battery → spins at ~22,200 RPM (dangerously fast).

> **Rule:** High-voltage setups (more cells) must be paired with **lower-KV motors** to avoid over-speeding. For heavy-lift drones, increase battery cells but decrease motor KV.

## Capacity (mAh – Milliampere-Hour)

**mAh** measures a battery's energy storage capacity — how much electric charge it can hold and how long it will power a device.

| mAh | Current Draw | Flight Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1,000 mAh | 1,000 mA (1A) | 1 hour |
| 1,000 mAh | 500 mA | 2 hours |
| 1,000 mAh | 250 mA | 4 hours |

- **Higher mAh** = longer flight time between charges.
- **Higher mAh** also means the drone can airlift a **heavier payload** for a longer duration.
- Common ratings: **2,200 mAh** or **3,300 mAh** for standard quadcopters.

> **Forensic Insight:** If a 2 kg drone brought to your lab has only a 1,000 mAh battery, you can deduce that its range is extremely limited — perhaps no more than 5 km. There is no need to assume it was launched from a distant cross-border location; it was likely flown by someone nearby. **Battery specifications alone allow you to infer the drone's flight capabilities and likely launch radius.**

## C-Rate (Charge/Discharge Speed)

The **C-rate** measures how quickly a battery can be safely charged or discharged relative to its maximum capacity.

**For a 10 Ah (10,000 mAh) battery:**

| C-Rate | Current | Time to Empty/Fill |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5C | 5A | 2 hours |
| **1C** | **10A** | **1 hour** |
| 2C | 20A | 30 minutes |

### Practical Applications

| C-Rate Range | Use Case |
|---|---|
| **High (2C – 10C+)** | Drones, EVs (fast charging), power tools, anti-drone interception systems |
| **Low (0.1C – 0.5C)** | Laptops, home electronics, off-grid battery banks (prolongs battery health) |

> **Forensic Insight:** If you find a drone with a battery rated at only 1C, it is not built for high speeds or aggressive manoeuvres. High-performance or anti-drone platforms require **high C-rate batteries (40C+)** for rapid discharge, though these are far more expensive.

# 2. Power Distribution Board (PDB)

A **Power Distribution Board** is a PCB designed to distribute power from the main flight battery to all components of the multirotor.

### Evolution

- **Originally:** Simple copper PCBs with one input and multiple output traces.
- **Now:** Manufacturers integrate **onboard voltage regulators** (visible as small ICs on the board) to prevent voltage spikes from throttle inputs that could damage sensitive electronics.

### Motor Capacity

- Each side of the PDB has **2 soldering-point pairs** (1 black + 1 red = 1 motor connection).
- 4 sides × 2 pairs = **8 motor connections** total.
- This allows building an **octacopter**, or a **coaxial quadcopter** (motors on top and bottom of each arm).

# Exam / Interview Points

### Battery
- **LiPo** = Lithium Polymer; each cell = **3.7V**
- **S count** = number of series cells (3S = 11.1V, 6S = 22.2V)
- **3S** is standard for quadcopters; **6S** for heavy agricultural drones
- **mAh** = capacity (higher = longer flight / heavier payload)
- **KV rating** = RPM per volt; high voltage → need lower KV motor
- **C-rate** = charge/discharge speed relative to capacity (1C = full discharge in 1 hour)
- High C-rate (40C+) = high-performance / anti-drone systems
- **Forensic use:** Battery specs → infer flight range and launch radius

### PDB
- Distributes battery power to all drone components
- Modern PDBs include **voltage regulators** to prevent spike damage
- Standard PDB supports up to **8 motors** (4 sides × 2 pairs)
- 1 solder pair (1 black + 1 red) = 1 motor connection
