For drone forensics, we must understand how a drone is assembled so we can:

1. **Disassemble** the drone properly
2. **Extract memory components** for forensic analysis

> **Forensic Principle:** Digital devices must be handed back to the issuing agency in the same state. This is why knowing assembly/disassembly is essential.

# 1. RC Flying (Remote Control Flying)

## What is RC Flying?

An **RC (Radio Control) transmitter** is a handheld device that sends pilot inputs to the drone's receiver. It operates on specific radio frequencies, allowing precise control over the drone's movement (pitch, roll, yaw, throttle) and additional features like flight modes and cameras.

### Communication Mediums

| Medium | Range | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Bluetooth** | ~30 meters | Toy drones (under ₹1,000), controlled via mobile phone |
| **Wi-Fi** | ~30 meters | Toy drones, controlled via mobile phone |
| **RF (Radio Frequency)** | Long range | Standard for all serious drones |

> **Note:** Both Bluetooth and Wi-Fi have very limited range (~30m) and are only suitable for toy drones. **RF is the standard** for all practical quadcopter operations.

### Real-World Note: Fiber-Optic Controlled Drones

In the Ukraine–Russia conflict, both sides now use **fiber-optic-controlled drones** to counter RF jamming (electronic warfare). A 5 kg fiber-optic spool provides a **30 km range**. India also uses RF jammers as countermeasures (e.g., around Rajpath during Independence Day parades).

## RC Transmitter (Tx)

### Channel Configurations

| Channels | Controls Available | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **3-Channel** | Throttle, Pitch, Yaw | Roll is **not** supported. Budget option only. |
| **4-Channel** | Throttle, Pitch, Roll, Yaw | Absolute minimum required for a quadcopter. |
| **6-Channel** | Throttle, Pitch, Roll, Yaw + 2 auxiliary | **Industry standard.** Extra channels for: arming, payload release, gimbal tilt. |

### Mode 1 vs Mode 2

| | Left Stick | Right Stick | Common In |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mode 1** | Pitch + Yaw | **Throttle** + Roll | Some countries |
| **Mode 2** | **Throttle** + Yaw | Pitch + Roll | **India (standard)** |

> **Mode 2 is preferred** for racing drones and high-speed flying — you set throttle (~70%) with your left thumb and actively manoeuvre with the right stick. Be careful when ordering: Mode 1 and Mode 2 transmitters are **not interchangeable**.

### Operating Frequencies

| Frequency | Status |
|---|---|
| **2.4 GHz** | **Standard for most consumer drones today** |
| 915 MHz / 868 MHz | Previously used for long-range FPV; now largely unavailable (cellular companies took over the band, phased out ~2018) |

### Communication Protocols

- **AFHDS** — The standard protocol used in modern RC transmitters and receivers.
- **ELRS (ExpressLRS)**, **Crossfire (TBS)** — Popular alternatives for FPV/long-range.
- **PPM** (Pulse Position Modulation) / **PWM** (Pulse Width Modulation) — Modulation modes.

> ⚠️ **The receiver and transmitter must use the same modulation mode.** If the receiver is set to PWM, the transmitter cannot be in PPM mode, and vice versa. They are usually sold as matching pairs.

### Telemetry

Modern transmitters provide **real-time feedback** from the drone:

- **Battery voltage** — alerts (blinking screen + sound) when voltage drops
- **Altitude**
- **GPS coordinates**

> Higher-end transmitters display telemetry directly on their LCD screen, which is useful when operating in remote/mountainous areas without a laptop.

## RC Receiver (Rx)

### Overview

The receiver takes inputs from the transmitter and passes them to the **Electronic Speed Controllers (ESCs)** and servos. Receivers can be 4, 6, or 10 channel (e.g., FS-i6X comes with a 10-channel receiver).

### Channel Mapping (Standard)

| Channel | Control |
|---|---|
| CH1 | Aileron (Roll) |
| CH2 | Elevator (Pitch) |
| CH3 | Throttle |
| CH4 | Rudder (Yaw) |

### 3-Pin Header Pinout

| Pin | Label | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Outer edge | **GND (-)** | Ground reference |
| Middle | **VCC (+)** | Power supply (usually 5V via ESC's BEC or dedicated receiver pack) |
| Top | **Signal (S)** | Sends PWM signal to ESC or servo |

> **Wiring Note:** Only Channel 1 typically requires all three wires. Other channels only need the **signal wire** connected, because the flight controller and ESCs share power through the board. This also avoids ground loop issues.

## Binding (Tx ↔ Rx Pairing)

### Why Binding is Critical

**Multiple drones of the same model** might be flying nearby using identical transmitters and receivers. Binding ensures your transmitter controls **only your drone**.

### The Bind Port

- Shares the same 3-pin voltage rail as other channel ports.
- Specifically wired to trigger **bind mode** when Signal and GND pins are **shorted**.
- The included **bind plug** connects Signal and GND pins.
- During normal operation, this port can also serve as a **secondary power input**.

### Binding Procedure (FlySky)

![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260704230552.png)

> Transmitters and receivers are usually **pre-bound from the factory**. Re-binding is needed when replacing a crashed receiver (~₹1,000) with a new one on an existing transmitter (~₹4,000).

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Turn ON the transmitter** (keep receiver OFF).
2. On the transmitter LCD → **Settings** → **System** → **Receiver Bind** (at bottom).
3. Select "Receiver Bind".
4. **Insert the bind plug** into the receiver's bind port.
5. **Power ON the receiver**.
6. The receiver's red LED will **flash 3 times repeatedly**.
7. The transmitter screen displays **"Bind OK"** — binding is successful.

> ⚠️ **The receiver must be powered on AFTER the transmitter.** If you power the receiver first, binding will fail. If the LED does not flash in the 3-blink pattern, repeat the process.

# Exam / Interview Points

- **RC** = Radio Control; standard medium is **RF** (not Bluetooth/Wi-Fi)
- **6-channel transmitter** = current industry standard
- 4 primary controls: **Throttle, Pitch, Roll, Yaw**
- **Mode 2** = throttle on left stick (Indian standard)
- **2.4 GHz** = standard operating frequency for consumer drones
- **AFHDS** = standard RC protocol; **PPM/PWM** must match between Tx and Rx
- **Telemetry** = real-time drone data sent back to transmitter (battery, altitude, GPS)
- **FlySky FS-i6** = popular standard transmitter (~₹4,500)
- Receiver connects to **ESCs** (motor speed) and **servos** (gimbal, payload)
- Channel mapping: CH1=Aileron, CH2=Elevator, CH3=Throttle, CH4=Rudder
- 3-pin header: GND (outer) → VCC (middle) → Signal (top)
- **BEC** = Battery Elimination Circuit (provides 5V to receiver from ESC)
- **Binding** = pairing Tx and Rx; **transmitter must be ON first**
- Bind plug = shorts Signal + GND on bind port to enter bind mode
- Fiber-optic drones used in Ukraine/Russia to defeat RF jamming (30 km range from 5 kg spool)
