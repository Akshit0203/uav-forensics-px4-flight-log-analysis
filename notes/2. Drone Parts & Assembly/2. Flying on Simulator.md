# Flying on Simulator

> If you have just purchased a transmitter and have no prior experience remote-controlling aircraft, it is **highly recommended to practice on a simulator first** to avoid crashing a costly quadcopter.

---

## Why Use a Simulator?

- Avoid expensive crashes while learning.
- Build **muscle memory** with your actual transmitter (weight, feel, switches).
- Learn throttle, pitch, roll, and yaw control in a risk-free environment.
- Once you are comfortable on the simulator, you are ready to begin the real build.

---

## Simulator Types

### 1. USB Simulator (Wired – Direct Connected)

A USB simulator cable (e.g., **FlySky FS-SM600**) plugs directly into your laptop and connects to flight simulator software.

**Setup:** Install simulator software → Connect USB cable to laptop → Practice flying.

### 2. Wireless RC Simulator (USB Dongle)

A **receiver dongle** plugs into your laptop, allowing you to practice wirelessly with your **actual transmitter**.

- **Advantage:** You practice with the real transmitter — builds muscle memory and confidence with its weight, feel, and switches.
- **Cost:** ₹1,900 – ₹3,000 for the wireless dongle.

### 3. Trainer Port Connection (Wired – Using Your Transmitter)

If you want to use your own transmitter but don't want to spend on a wireless dongle, use the **trainer port** at the back of your transmitter.

- Simulator cables come with various connectors tailored to different transmitter brands:

| Brand | Connector Type |
|---|---|
| **FlySky** | Round trainer-port connector (**current standard**) |
| RadioLink, WFLY | Brand-specific cables |
| Futaba | Square-type connector (older, less common today) |
| JR | Different style connector (older, less common today) |

> Futaba and JR were once very popular but are less common today. FlySky with a round trainer-port connector is the current standard.

# Exam / Interview Points

- **Always practice on a simulator** before flying a real quadcopter
- Three simulator connection methods: **USB cable**, **wireless dongle**, **trainer port**
- Wireless dongle is best for building muscle memory with your actual transmitter
- **FlySky FS-SM600** = standard 6-channel USB simulator
- Compatible software: Reflex XTR, RealFlight, AeroFly, PhoenixRC, APD
- FlySky round trainer-port connector = current standard
- Older brands (Futaba, JR) used square / brand-specific connectors
